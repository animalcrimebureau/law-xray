Animal Crime Bureau, Working Paper No. 2, ACB/WP/002

Law X-ray

Choose any definition or provision of Egypt's 2023 law on dangerous animals to see the text it shares with the Emirati law of 2016 and the Bahraini law of 2026. The laws are shown in their original Arabic. Shared words are marked in red.

How the tool works

A word in red belongs to a sequence of five words that appears, word for word, in both texts. The marks are computed from the official Arabic texts after removing vowel marks and punctuation and treating different spellings of the same letter as one letter, by the method of Working Paper No. 2. Qatar's law of 2019, drafted separately, shares about 1 percent with each of the other laws and serves as the baseline.
